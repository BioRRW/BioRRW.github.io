Designed Around Your Space & Routine

Coffee tables take more daily impact than almost any piece of living room furniture—from feet resting on edges to spilled drinks and kids' toy cars.


Instead of disposable veneer boxes that chip at the corners, RW Custom Furniture crafts solid hardwood coffee tables tailored to your room layout. Whether you need integrated storage drawers with hand-cut dovetails or a lower shelf for books, every build is engineered to look incredible and withstand decades of daily life.

Custom Features & Options

  • ✓ Solid Hardwood Tops: Hard Maple, Red Oak, Walnut, or Cherry.
  • ✓ Integrated Storage: Soft-close concealed drawers or slatted lower shelves.
  • ✓ Child-Friendly Finishes: Pure tung oil & hardwax sealants with zero toxic off-gassing.
  • ✓ Custom Dimensions: Tailored height and footprint to match your sofa setup perfectly.
