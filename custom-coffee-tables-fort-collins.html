Designed Around Your Living Room & Daily Routine

Coffee tables take more daily impact than almost any piece of furniture—from feet resting on edges to spilled coffee mugs and kids' toy cars. Mass-produced veneer boxes chip easily and wobble within a few seasons.


At RW Custom Furniture, I handcraft solid hardwood coffee tables tailored to your room layout. Whether you want an organic live edge slab, clean mid-century lines, or matching companion furniture like TV stands and side tables, every piece is built using rock-solid joinery and non-toxic, zero-VOC natural oil finishes.

The Custom Living Room Standard

  • ✓ 100% Solid Timber & Live Edge Slabs: Hard Maple, Red Oak, Walnut, and organic natural slab options.
  • ✓ Zero-VOC Natural Oils: Non-toxic, food-safe finishes that penetrate deep without chemical off-gassing.
  • ✓ Rock-Solid Joinery: Built using mortise-and-tenons, sliding dovetails, and gold-standard hardware.
  • ✓ Cohesive Sets: Custom dimensions tailored to match your sofa height, media center, and room proportions.
