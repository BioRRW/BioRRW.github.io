Built for Daily Life, Not Disposable Consumerism

Standard retail dining tables look fine in a showroom, but within two years of real-family use, veneered particle board peels, clip-on legs wobble, and harsh chemical topcoats turn cloudy from warm dishes.


At RW Custom Furniture, every dining table is built to order in Fort Collins using hand-selected regional hardwoods like Red Oak, Hard Maple, and Black Walnut. I utilize rock-solid joinery techniques paired with gold-standard structural hardware to ensure your table handles decades of real life without sagging or shaking.

The Custom Dining Standard

  • ✓ 100% Solid Hardwood: No MDF, paper veneers, or particle board.
  • ✓ Zero-VOC Natural Oils: Food-safe finishes that penetrate deep into the grain.
  • ✓ Rock-Solid Joinery & Gold-Standard Hardware: Traditional joinery methods paired with heavy-duty fasteners and best-practice structural hardware.
  • ✓ Custom Dimensions: Tailored to fit your exact room proportions and seating requirements.
