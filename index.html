My Focus & Approach

I build custom furniture and architectural built-ins designed to solve spatial problems. My goal is to make your daily routine smoother by filling specific gaps in your home.

Custom Built-ins for Daily Efficiency
A well-engineered mudroom or custom closet is essential. It should allow you to enter and leave your home without feeling hampered or annoyed. I design spaces that efficiently manage your family's gear, giving you a frustration-free place to "drop everything" after a long day.

Heirloom Quality Over Disposable Culture
I reject the cycle of mass-produced furniture that ends up broken on a local marketplace in two years. Instead, I use premium hardwoods and rock-solid traditional joinery to engineer pieces that take a beating. From kitchen tables sturdy enough for kids' forts to dressers that handle decades of slammed drawers, I build solutions designed for real life.
