No Particle Board or MDF

Cheap engineered wood swells with humidity, sags under weight, and crumbles. I use 100% solid, hand-selected hardwoods.

Zero Chemical Off-Gassing

Mass-produced furniture off-gasses formaldehyde and VOCs. I finish every build with pure, non-toxic tung oil, shellac, and natural sealants.

Traditional Craft & Gold-Standard Hardware

No plastic brackets or flimsy cam-locks. Whether cutting traditional joinery or installing heavy-duty soft-close slides and structural fasteners, I use the absolute best hardware and methods for every build.

Heirloom Quality

Built to stop the wasteful cycle of flat-pack furniture. From custom dining tables for family dinners to handcrafted coffee tables and nursery cribs, I build pieces that last generations.
