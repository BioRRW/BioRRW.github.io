About Me

With seven years of professional experience in residential construction—handling everything from framing and roofing to meticulous finish work—I bring a deep understanding of structural integrity to every project.


Today, my true passion lies in fine woodworking. I specialize in building custom furniture utilizing robust techniques like sliding dovetails and mortise-and-tenon joints. Whether I'm hand-cutting joints with a 240mm Dozuki saw or applying natural tung oil to a hard maple dresser, I focus on the meticulous details that make a piece truly special.
