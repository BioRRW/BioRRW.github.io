About The Builder

My foundation in the trades began from the ground up, with seven years of experience in residential construction spanning masonry, framing, and meticulous finish work. That structural intuition informs everything I build today.


Operating out of Northern Colorado, my focus is strictly on fine furniture design rather than volume-based production. I specialize in designing and building custom pieces using premium materials like locally sourced oak and maple stock. By utilizing traditional techniques—from cutting precision dovetails with a Japanese Dozuki saw to applying hand-rubbed tung oil and shellac finishes—I ensure every piece is structurally flawless and uniquely yours.
