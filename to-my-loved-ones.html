Custom Builds at Material Cost

Welcome! Whether you are a friend or a family member I’m truly honored and thankful you’re here.

Please feel free to share this link with your own friends and family—because in the end, we are all family.

How It Works

1. Bring Me Your Ideas

Whether you need a major statement piece like a dining table, coffee table, or built-in bookshelf—or smaller home accents like serving trays, cutting boards, or layout pieces—send me your sketches, dimensions, or links to designs you love (Pinterest, Wayfair, or photos).

2. Complimentary 3D CAD Design

I will translate your idea into a full 3D CAD model. We'll refine the proportions, joinery, and wood species together until you can clearly picture the exact piece in your room before a single board is cut.

3. Raw Material Cost Only

I calculate the exact board footage of hardwood (hard maple, white oak, walnut, cherry, etc.) and hardware required. You cover the raw material costs—no markup, zero labor fee.

4. Handcrafted Build & Delivery

I'll build your piece right here in my Fort Collins shop using traditional joinery methods built to last a lifetime, then deliver and install it in your home.
